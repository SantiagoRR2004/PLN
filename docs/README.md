# Natural Language Processing

## Overview

These are three projects for a subject called "Procesamiento del Lenguaje Natural" (Natural Language Processing) that accounted for 15%, 15% and 10% of the final grade, respectively. They were made by a team of 2 people.

The first was implementing [Byte-pair Encoding](../P1/). It was submitted on Thursday, the 16th of October of 2025, and earned a grade of 10 out of 10 points.

The second was coding [Word2vec](../P2/) with `Skipgram` but we also implemented `CBOW`. It was submitted on Tuesday, the 20th of November of 2025, and earned a grade of 9 out of 10 points.

The final project was [Sentiment Analysis](../P3/) with a basic [classifier](../P3/p3_classifier.py) and [fine-tuning a pre-trained model](../P3/p3_finetune.py). It was submitted on Thursday, the 18th of December of 2025, and earned a grade of 10 out of 10 points.
